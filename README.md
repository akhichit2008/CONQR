### Corporate–NGO Quality Responsibility

> **END-TO-END Enterprise Framework for Impactful Partnerships**

CONQR helps CSR teams move from **"Which NGO should we work with?"** to **"Here are the most Suitable & Impactful NGO partners, and here's why."**

Instead of relying on manual research, fragmented NGO information, or keyword-based discovery, CONQR converts a corporate CSR requirement into a structured profile, filters incompatible organizations, evaluates eligible NGOs across multiple dimensions, and produces an explainable ranked shortlist.

---

## The Problem

Corporate CSR teams frequently face:

- Difficulty identifying suitable NGO partners
- Fragmented information about NGO capabilities and projects
- Mismatch between CSR objectives and NGO expertise
- Difficulty comparing NGOs using standardized criteria
- Challenges finding NGOs operating in specific regions
- Manual and time-consuming partner selection

The result?

**More research. More coordination. Less confidence in the final partnership decision.**

---

# Our Solution

CONQR acts as an **intelligent matchmaking and decision-support layer** between corporations and NGOs.

A CSR team provides a project requirement such as:

> *"We need an NGO for a rural digital literacy program for women in East Africa, with a budget of $50,000 for 7 months."*

CONQR transforms this into structured requirements, eliminates NGOs that fail mandatory constraints, scores the remaining candidates across multiple dimensions, and ranks the best-fit partners.

### The result

Instead of manually evaluating dozens of organizations:

```text
CSR Requirement
       ↓
Requirement Processing
       ↓
Hard Constraint Filtering
       ↓
Multi-Dimensional Scoring
       ↓
NGO Ranking
       ↓
Top NGO Partners
       ↓
Explainable Comparison

```
